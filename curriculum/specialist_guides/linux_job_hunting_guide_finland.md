PRO INSIGHT: Your single biggest interview advantage: you have REAL projects to talk about. When asked 'tell
me about something you built and deployed', most juniors have a tutorial. You have GoOdo — a real app you
designed, built, containerised, and run on a real server. Walk through the decisions: why systemd, why nginx, how
you handled HTTPS and backups, what broke and how you fixed it. That story, told honestly, beats any certification.
Practice explaining it in 2 minutes, then in 10.

TIP: FAST WIN: For the 'diagnose a slow server' question (very common), memorise the layered approach from
Volume 4: check load/CPU (top, uptime), then memory (free -h, is it swapping?), then disk (df, iostat, full or slow?),
then network. Naming this systematic method out loud instantly signals you think like an operator, not a guesser.
